# Measures of 26 November 2026

Everything measured on 26 November, in one place, so that none of it has to be run again. Each line says the machine, the
build, the scene and how it was counted. "Play fps" is the median of the log's `vk: N presents, F fps` lines after
`play: began`, the first third left out (the bench file's own fps starts before play and is not usable). Hand-played
figures depend on how the owner moved: they are indications, his eye is the verdict.

Machines: main PC = Ryzen 7 7800X3D, RTX 5070 12 GB; PC 3050 = i5-10400F, RTX 3050 8 GB; laptop = Ryzen 7 5700U, Vega 8.
Builds: int1126f = main cbcaf5206 (batch A); int1126g = main cb6c58a90 (crash fix and five Longshot ports); int1126h =
int1126g + the steady pick + the VRAM floors port (not merged); expo3b..expo3e = branch `fix/expo3-rbclasses` (not merged).

### What is proven and what is refuted

- The read-back ring's pick skipped and repeated samples when a worker's read ran after the main thread's present
  (delivered tags 1 1 2 0 1 1). The steady pick (`rb_ring::pick_steady`, switch `HL_RB_PICK`) removes that: delivered-tag
  steps of 1 in 5843 of 5845 reads. It cleans the scripted pans, not what the owner sees.
- The meter's GPU work is aligned per frame: one clear and one dispatch for each of the two meter programs, every frame.
  Samples that looked inflated are true measures of brighter frames: the picture brightens first, the meter measures
  it, the exposure dips three frames later.
- Block A of the meter (0x113c88b000) reads zero most of the time in the day Field (held by `RX_VK_METER_HOLD`): the
  program's own result there, not a misalignment.
- The early read-back of the 0x3980 block: the two ranges (113b623000, 113b628000) alternate by frame; each job writes
  one range with 8 bindings. The exact read (verify mode against join 3) holds the block as it stood before the 7th
  writer, program 3018813b775edcb6 (the 8th is fd958faab99f20a2); the copies behind writers 1 to 6 are identical. The
  default serves the last copy: 300 of 301 reads differ from the exact value in about 1500 of 3680 words. Bind order
  equals command order (66 jobs 6 + 2 bindings by thread, 7 jobs 8 + 0).
- Serving the copy before writer 7 on both paths (expo3e) brings the mismatches to 177 of 7969 (2 %), and the flicker
  stays and the grass flickers. So the 0x3980 block's value is NOT the cure. What join 3 and the order thread off change
  beyond that block is not identified.
- Not the limiter's held presents (as many steps after a hold as elsewhere), not the lag (2, 3, 4 alike), not specific to
  the 60 limit (as many at 83 fps uncapped).

## 4. The Market pot storeroom (PC 3050, build of 424cd8ef8 with `RX_VK_LOG_IMAGES=1`)

Per 600 presents, 66 s windows, two runs each:

| Situation | Depth images made new (reused) | Colour targets new | Distinct depth sizes | fps | Frames over 33 ms a minute | Longest ms |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Market square, camera turning | 1798 (1791) / 1792 (1752) | 365 / 358 | 745 | 57.2 / 56.4 | 5.3 / 10.8 | 50 / 42 |
| Room, still | 38 (3) / 52 (7) | 3 | 12-18 | 69.0 / 70.4 | 2.7 / 0.9 | 52 |
| Room, moving | 1368 (620) / 1065 (503) | 119 / 99 | 600-770 | 46.3 / 61.4 | 64 / 47 | 109 / 90 |

Depth is 80-90 % of the images made; fixed families (384x384, 640x360, 768x432, 1536x864) and unique squares 1-3 px
apart. Creating and destroying them costs about 0.9 ms a present (5 % of a frame). The square makes more of them than the
room and hitches less: the churn is a cost, not shown to be the cause of the stutter there. The tester's VRAM episode
(94-98 % of the allowance) was not reproduced on the 8 GB card (86-91 %). No constant upload in the room (0.0 MB a frame
still). Exposure steady with nothing moving (luminance 40.4, drift +7 % in 43 s).

The tester's log (RTX 3080 Ti, 10 minutes, 36,000 presents): render-target images made new 6.6-9.0 a present in the room
against 0.3-1.0 elsewhere; one stutter episode in the first 10 s (VRAM 10539 of 10784 MB, memory blocks given back in 77
and 81 ms before slow presents of 109 and 89 ms); 4 STALE reads, all inside the three load windows; in Hyrule Field 3284
textures retired in one pass, then 14.4 GB uploaded again.

## 5. The texture cache's eviction (PC 3050, branch `fix/evict-to-budget`, not merged)

Tour of zone loads, play fps, `RX_VK_EVICT_TARGET_PCT`:

| Config | Off (old) | 97 | 88 | 75 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Natural budget: fps | 64.2 | 62.0 | 62.8 | 64.2 |
| Natural budget: slow Hyrule visits | 0 of 4 | 5 of 5 | 1 of 2 | 0 of 3 |
| Commit rule off, budget 2560: fps | 70.3 | 64.5 / 67.7 | 67.8 | 69.1 |
| Commit rule off: mean textures retired a wave | 1034-1147 | 368-417 | 586 | 911 |

Hyrule Field on that card has two states per visit: 9 ms of GPU a frame (75-78 fps) or 18 ms (43-47 fps), until the next
warp. In the slow state the write-back copies cost 0.9 ms each for 46-58 MB a frame, against 0.03 ms for 17-22 MB. At
the natural budget about 1100 of 2000 passes are squeezed by the commit rule, so no target changes the largest wave
(1481-1541). No target keeps small waves without the slowdown on this card.
