# v0.4.0 per-zone benchmark: HeyListen, NX2navi and Longshot (2026-11-15/16)

In-game frame rates of the three Ocarina of Time PC ports that build from the player's own Switch 2 dump, on two PCs.

**Method.** 2560x1440 output, 3D scene at 100 %, no upscaler, no frame generation, no HDR, uncapped (HeyListen at
`HOS_FPS_LIMIT=360`), audio off. Link stands idle in each zone, loaded from the same saves. PresentMon 2.6 records every
present of the game's process; the window is the 40 s after each build's "in play" marker (no title, loading or shader
preparation in it). Three runs per build and zone, interleaved; the tables use runs 2 and 3 (run 1 is the first visit).
Mean fps = frames / time; 1 % and 0.1 % low = 1000 / the mean frame time of the slowest 1 % (0.1 %) of the frames.
Table B leaves out the first 3 s of each window (the builds' "in play" markers come at different moments of the load's
end); table A is the whole window. Builds: HeyListen v0.4.0 (main 4af23672, built like a player's build, with the
optimization profile), NX2navi 0.11.0-rc4, Longshot v3.0.0-rc.4 (built from its tag with the same tools).

**Read these with the differences in mind.**
- Present modes differ: NX2navi and Longshot immediate (no V-Sync), HeyListen mailbox (its default: no tearing).
- PC 3050: six HeyListen runs (Hyrule Field r2-r3, Lost Woods, Sacred Forest Meadow r1) ran immediate. Two
  HeyListen runs lost the GPU (Graveyard r1, Inside the Deku Tree r2): a known issue of this release, being fixed.
- Main PC: the series was stopped after 13 zones; the PC was in use meanwhile and most runs of all three builds
  ran windowed (2560x1440 picture in a 1280x720 window). NVIDIA's own shader cache sat at its 1 GB default while the
  three games ran in turn, so they evicted each other's pipelines: HeyListen's start-up shaders took 6-97 s there
  (3.7 s on the PC 3050) and some of its steady-play windows hold multi-second frames that the PC 3050 does not
  show; Longshot froze for seconds in a few runs too. NX2navi ran with its full performance overlay in the main PC table from the Great
  Fairy Fountain to Lon Lon Ranch (its figures there are likely a little low). The PC 3050 table is the cleaner of the two.
- The Great Fairy Fountain: four files of the dump used are damaged; NX2navi replaces three of them with stand-ins and
  may draw less there.
- A cell marked * has one valid run instead of two.
